Before you begin: test rules & data information

Scoring. +1 for a correct answer, −0.25 for an incorrect answer and 0 for an unattempted question. Every question has one scored answer.

Timing. 90 minutes. The test auto-submits when time runs out. Refreshing or leaving the tab does not reset the clock.

Review. You can navigate freely, clear an answer, or mark it for review. An answered question marked for review still counts toward your score.

Results. Correct, incorrect and unattempted questions all include explanations. Shared ranks appear only after a successful Sheet submission.

Ranking rule. Higher net score first; ties use less time taken, then earlier submission. Overall and category ranks include submitted attempts only.

Privacy & limitations. The Sheet is private to its owner; the public leaderboard excludes phone numbers. Mobile registration is not OTP-verified authentication. This client-side practice test is not a secure, proctored examination.
